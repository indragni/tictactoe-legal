Support

Get help

  • Open the app → Settings → look for “Support” / “Contact” if available.
  • Include your iPhone model, iOS version, and a short description of the issue.

Troubleshooting

  • Force close and reopen the app.
  • Restart your device.
  • Check permissions in iOS Settings (Camera/Photos/etc.).
